\subsection{Overall comparison}

\Cref{tab:main} reports mean scores and mean ranks over the \NTasks{} tasks and
\cref{fig:cd} the corresponding critical-difference diagrams. The Friedman test
rejects equal performance for log loss ($p=\FriedmanPLogloss$), ROC-AUC
($p=\FriedmanPRocauc$) and accuracy ($p=\FriedmanPAccuracy$). On log loss, NEXUS
has mean rank \NexusRankLogloss{} (place \NexusPlaceLogloss{} of 15); the best
other method is \BestOtherNameLogloss{} (\BestOtherRankLogloss). On accuracy,
NEXUS ranks \NexusRankAccuracy{} (place \NexusPlaceAccuracy{}), and on ROC-AUC
\NexusRankRocauc{} (place \NexusPlaceRocauc{}).

\subsection{Pairwise comparisons}

\Cref{tab:pairwise} and \cref{fig:forest} compare NEXUS with every other method
task by task. \Cref{fig:scatter} shows the per-task log loss against the three
strongest references. Against AutoGluon with the same per-fold time budget,
NEXUS wins on \WinAutoGluonLogloss{}, ties on \TieAutoGluonLogloss{} and loses on
\LossAutoGluonLogloss{} tasks in log loss (Holm-corrected $p=\PAutoGluonLogloss$;
$\Delta R^2 = \DRTwoAutoGluonLogloss$\,pp, 95\% CI
[\DRTwoLoAutoGluonLogloss, \DRTwoHiAutoGluonLogloss]), and wins/ties/loses on
\WinAutoGluonAccuracy/\TieAutoGluonAccuracy/\LossAutoGluonAccuracy{} tasks in
accuracy ($p=\PAutoGluonAccuracy$). Against its own predecessor NEXUS~v1 the
stacking layer wins on \WinNEXUSvOneLogloss{} and loses on
\LossNEXUSvOneLogloss{} tasks in log loss ($p=\PNEXUSvOneLogloss$;
$\Delta R^2=\DRTwoNEXUSvOneLogloss$\,pp). Against the tuned LightGBM baseline
the record is \WinLightGBMtunedLogloss/\TieLightGBMtunedLogloss/\LossLightGBMtunedLogloss{}
($p=\PLightGBMtunedLogloss$; mean relative log-loss reduction
\RelLightGBMtunedLogloss\%, 95\% CI [\CILoLightGBMtunedLogloss, \CIHiLightGBMtunedLogloss]).

\subsection{Ablations}

\Cref{tab:ablation} and \cref{fig:ablation} ablate NEXUS~v1 on the same saved
predictions. Removing temperature scaling changes log loss by
\RelAblNEXUSvOnewocalibrationLogloss\% (CI [\CILoAblNEXUSvOnewocalibrationLogloss,
\CIHiAblNEXUSvOnewocalibrationLogloss]); replacing selection by choosing the best
model by cross-validation costs \RelAblBestmodelbyCVLogloss\%; restricting the
library to the three GBDTs costs \RelAblNEXUSvOneGBDTsonlyLogloss\%; uniform
averaging of all members costs \RelAblUniformaverageLogloss\%; and a
logistic-regression stacker costs \RelAblStackingLRLogloss\%. Positive values mean
the full method is better.

\subsection{Ensemble composition, size and calibration}

On average NEXUS selects \MeanMembers{} of its 10 candidates per fold
(\cref{fig:weights}). Greedy selection converges quickly (\cref{fig:size}): the
gain from 25 to 100 iterations is negligible. The fitted temperature has median
\TempMedian{} (IQR \TempQOne--\TempQThree). \Cref{fig:reliability} shows pooled
reliability diagrams.
