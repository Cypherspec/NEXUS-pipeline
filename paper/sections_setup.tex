\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Tasks.} We use OpenML-CC18 \citep{bischl2021openml,vanschoren2013openml},
72 curated classification tasks. Before running any experiment we fixed an
inclusion rule for a 4-core CPU budget: at most 12{,}000 instances and 300
features. This keeps \NTasks{} tasks (with 2--11 classes, 500--11{,}055 rows, up to
256 features; Appendix~\ref{app:tasks}) and excludes the image-like and largest
tasks (e.g.\ MNIST, CIFAR-10, Devnagari, letter, adult). Results therefore speak
to small and medium tabular data.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Every method is trained and evaluated on the official
OpenML 10-fold cross-validation splits of each task (\NFolds{} train/test pairs in
total), so all methods see identical data. Within a training fold, NEXUS and
every ensemble baseline use only that fold for model fitting, weight
selection and calibration. Seeds equal the fold index.

\paragraph{Baselines.} (i) Each of the 8 library algorithms, fit once on the full
training fold. (ii) \emph{LightGBM (tuned)}: random search over 20
configurations (trees, learning rate, leaves, minimum child samples, row and
column subsampling, L2), scored by log loss on a stratified 20\% holdout and
refit on the full training fold. (iii) Three ensembling strategies that share
NEXUS's exact bagged base predictions and OOF matrix, isolating the combination
step: \emph{Uniform average} of all 8 bagged models; \emph{Best model by CV},
the bagged algorithm with the lowest OOF log loss (standard model selection);
and \emph{Stacking (LR)}, a multinomial logistic-regression meta-learner on
OOF log-probabilities \citep{wolpert1992stacked,breiman1996stacked}.

\paragraph{Metrics.} Log loss (primary; a strictly proper scoring rule),
ROC-AUC (binary, or macro one-vs-rest), accuracy, balanced accuracy, Brier score
and top-label expected calibration error (ECE, 15 bins). Scores are averaged over
the 10 folds of each task.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Following \citet{demsar2006statistical} we rank methods per
task, test for any difference with the Friedman test (Iman--Davenport $F$
form), and draw critical-difference diagrams with the Nemenyi test at
$\alpha{=}0.05$. For NEXUS versus each competitor we report win/tie/loss counts
over tasks, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm correction across competitors
\citep{garcia2008extension}, and the mean per-task \emph{relative improvement}:
$(L_\text{other}-L_\text{NEXUS})/L_\text{other}$ for losses and the relative
reduction of $1-S$ for scores $S$ (AUC, accuracy), with 95\% percentile bootstrap
confidence intervals over tasks (10{,}000 resamples).

\paragraph{Correctness check.} All ensemble variants and ablations are recomputed
offline from saved OOF and test predictions. Recomputed NEXUS predictions match
those produced by the estimator online to within \RecomputeDiff{} (max absolute
difference in probability), confirming that the analysis evaluates the shipped
code.
