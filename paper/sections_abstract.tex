\begin{abstract}
We present NEXUS, a deterministic ensemble classifier for tabular data that
cross-fits a fixed library of eight diverse learners, adds a two-model stacking
layer, selects sparse convex ensemble weights by greedy forward selection on
out-of-fold predictions, and calibrates the mixture with a single temperature.
All design decisions were made on 16 development datasets disjoint from the
evaluation suite, and the design was frozen and publicly committed before
evaluation. On \NTasks{} OpenML-CC18 tasks
with their official 10-fold splits, NEXUS achieves mean log-loss rank
\NexusRankLogloss{} among 15 methods, including eight single models with default
hyperparameters, a tuned LightGBM, three ensembling strategies on identical base
predictions, and AutoGluon given the same per-fold compute budget.
%RESULT-SENTENCE%
We also report negative results: larger model portfolios, alternative weight
optimisers and repeated cross-fitting did not help, despite a measurable,
learner-dependent bias in out-of-fold estimates of bagged models. Code, cached
predictions and a one-command reproduction script are provided.
\end{abstract}
