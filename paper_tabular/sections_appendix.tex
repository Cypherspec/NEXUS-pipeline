\section{Tuned LightGBM search space}
\label{app:tuning}
Random search over 20 configurations: number of trees $\in\{100,200,400,800\}$;
learning rate log-uniform in $[0.01, 0.3]$; leaves $\in\{7,15,31,63,127\}$; minimum
child samples $\in\{5,10,20,50\}$; row subsample $U(0.5,1)$ (every iteration);
column subsample $U(0.4,1)$; L2 regularisation log-uniform in $[10^{-3},10]$.
Configurations are scored by log loss on a stratified 20\% holdout of the
training fold and the best is refit on the full training fold.
